How It Works

A transparent, deterministic 4-stage pipeline designed for human-in-the-loop recruiting.

01

Multi-Format Ingestion

Extracts clean text and structural layout from PDF, Microsoft Word DOCX, and TXT resumes while stripping personal identifiers.

02

Skill Taxonomy Normalization

Maps 50+ technology aliases (e.g. JS → JavaScript, K8s → Kubernetes) and distinguishes Required from Preferred qualifications.

03

TF-IDF & Cosine Similarity

Projects documents into a shared sublinear vector space, computing geometric cosine similarity scores with mathematical rigor.

04

Multi-Factor Alignment & Audit

Combines text relevance, skill coverage, role tenure, degree discipline, and an 8-point ATS parseability diagnostic.

Built for Modern Hiring Teams

Everything you need to triage, analyze, and shortlist candidates with confidence.

Sub-15ms Local Inference

Zero cloud latency and zero expensive LLM token costs. Runs entirely locally on your infrastructure with deterministic scoring.

8-Point ATS Parseability Audit

Identifies unparseable formatting, missing contact info, date span ambiguities, and text corruption before human review.

Side-by-Side Comparison

Compare top candidates across 6 quantitative dimensions and missing skills in a compact matrix modal.

1-Click CSV Export

Download complete structured rankings, sub-metric breakdowns, and explainability narratives for ATS sync.

Explainable AI Evidence

Clear natural language rationales explaining exactly why a candidate received their screening score.

Demographic Neutrality

Protected characteristics (gender, age, race, photo, marital status, address) are strictly excluded from ranking calculations.
